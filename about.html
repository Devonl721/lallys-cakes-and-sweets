Our story · EST. 2012

Delightful & elegant since 2012

Lally’s Cakes & Sweets are delightful and elegant. We’re experienced bakers who can meet all of your party dessert specialties — with over 100 flavors.

A family bakery at the market

Since 2012, Lally’s Cakes & Sweets has been baking for celebrations across Berks County. You’ll find us at the Shillington Farmers Market in Shillington, PA.

We’re known for party dessert specialties — custom cakes, cupcakes, cookies, and trays that look elegant and taste homemade. Whether it’s a birthday, shower, holiday table, or office treat, we help you choose from a flavor lineup of over 100 options.

Stop by our stand at the market when we’re open, or reach out by phone or Facebook Message to place an order.

Our price range is approachable ($$) — we believe everyone should be able to enjoy something sweet.
